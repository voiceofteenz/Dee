🎫 Queue-Powered Ticket System

This page explains where queues are used in the real world, why they are necessary, and demonstrates one application: a bank / clinic ticket line built with a queue (First-In, First-Out).

2. Why Queues Are Necessary

  • Fairness: FIFO guarantees first come, first served, so nobody is skipped.
  • Different speeds: producers (users) and consumers (printer, server, clerk) work at different rates. The queue holds the backlog between them.
  • Order preservation: typed characters, packets and transactions only make sense in sequence.
  • Efficiency: enqueue and dequeue are fast operations at opposite ends, with no searching for the oldest item.
  • Asynchronous work: a task is submitted now and processed later by a worker.

A stack (last-in, first-out) would serve the newest customer first, which is the wrong behavior for all of the cases above.
